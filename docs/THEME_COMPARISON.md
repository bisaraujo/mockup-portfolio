## Theme Details

### Dark Theme
**Best for:**
- 🖥️ Tech documentation
- 📱 Modern apps/games
- 🔧 Tool wikis
- 📚 General knowledge bases
- 💼 Professional content

**Design Elements:**
- Clean lines
- Minimal decoration
- High contrast
- Sans-serif typography
- Blue accent color
- Flat design

**Mood:**
- Professional
- Modern
- Efficient
- Tech-forward
- Neutral

---

### Fantasy Theme
**Best for:**
- 🐉 Fantasy RPG games (D&D, Pathfinder)
- ⚔️ Medieval settings
- 🏰 Castle & kingdom lore
- 📖 Story world-building
- 🎭 Fiction wikis
- Fantasy maps & locations

**Design Elements:**
- Golden borders
- Ornamental patterns
- Warm shadows
- Serif typography
- Brass/gold accents
- Textured appearance

**Mood:**
- Immersive
- Atmospheric
- Ancient/Historic
- Magical
- Storytelling

## Quick Switch Guide

**To enable Fantasy Theme:**
1. Click 🏰 Fantasy button in header
2. Page instantly transforms
3. Preference saved automatically

**To return to Dark Theme:**
1. Click 🌙 Dark button in header
2. Returns to modern style
3. Preference saved automatically
